## Paper Method Summary

### Doublet detection
- Tool: Scrublet
- `expected_doublet_rate=0.06`, `min_counts=2`, `min_cells=3`, `min_gene_variability_pctl=85`, `n_prin_comps=30`
- Applied to merged cohort

### Batch correction
- Tool: Harmony (`harmonypy`)
- Applied after PCA; run separately on tumor sub-dataset and background+healthy sub-dataset
- `batch_key=sample`

### Clustering
- Leiden on 15-dimensional harmonised PCA space, resolution 1.0
- Visualisation: UMAP

### Differential expression (marker genes)
- Wilcoxon signed-rank test + Bonferroni correction
- `min_pct=0.30` (gene expressed in ≥30% of cells), `logfc_threshold=0`

### Differential expression (condition-level)
- Pseudobulk via py_DESeq2 + patient as covariate
- Threshold: `median(padj) ≤ 0.05` and `|median(logFC)| ≥ 1`

### Cell type annotation
- Manual annotation from canonical marker genes; scArches label transfer for cross-subset consistency

## Deliberate Differences from Paper

### 1. sparse_exact PCA engine (engineering, not science)

The paper uses standard dense Scanpy PCA. Our pipeline uses `sparse_exact` (TruncatedSVD via ARPACK on the sparse matrix directly), which avoids in-memory densification of the 884k-cell expression matrix.

**Scientific equivalence**: TruncatedSVD on sparse input is mathematically identical to PCA on mean-centred data when applied to HVG-filtered expression. No information is lost; eigenspace is equivalent up to floating-point tolerance.

**Why**: Dense PCA at 884k × 30k would require >265 GB RAM. `sparse_exact` keeps peak memory under available capacity on the GPU node.

**Documentation**: `# densify-allowed: <reason>` annotation required at any deliberate densification call-site; CI grep-ban rejects unmarked `toarray()`/`todense()` calls.

### 2. Capability flag system (engineering)

The paper does not describe a mode-switching system. Our codebase exposes per-module capability flags rather than a monolithic `--scale-mode` toggle. This replaces the historical `scale_mode` large/massive distinction for analytical decisions.

**Why**: Finer-grained control over which modules run; cleaner code path; no analytical difference from paper's single-path execution.

### 3. Per-sample (grouped) doublet detection

Paper implies cohort-level Scrublet at ~900k cells. We run Scrublet per sample (grouped), then aggregate.

**Why**: Intra-droplet doublet semantics are preserved per sample; avoids holding ~265 GB dense PCA for merged doublet scoring. Parameters (`n_prin_comps=30`, `expected_doublet_rate=0.06`) match exactly.

**Scientific defensibility**: Biologically more conservative (within-sample co-encapsulation is the correct doublet model). Not a downgrade.

### 4. 24 patients, not 25 (QC)

One patient was lost during QC filtering (minimum cells-per-sample threshold). Final cohort: 24 patients (tumor sub-dataset) + 2 healthy donors.

## Audit Verdict

All analytical parameters match the paper. The three deliberate differences (sparse_exact engine, capability flags, per-sample doublets) are engineering improvements that preserve or strengthen biological validity. The 24-patient cohort is a QC outcome, not a methodological divergence.
